# Spanish for your home visit

Short phrases an English-speaking visitor can use with the building porter, the Vela doctor and a pharmacy. The Vela doctor already receives a full briefing in Spanish, so these are only to help the visit run smoothly. Pronunciation hints are approximate.

## With the building porter (portería)
- "Viene un médico de Vela Night Care para el apartamento..." (A doctor from Vela Night Care is coming to apartment...) — VYEN-eh oon MEH-dee-koh deh VEH-lah...
- "Por favor, déjelo subir." (Please let him or her come up.) — por fah-VOR, DEH-heh-loh soo-BEER.
- "Estoy enfermo / enferma." (I am sick, said by a man / a woman.) — es-TOY en-FER-moh / en-FER-mah.

## With the doctor
- "Gracias por venir." (Thank you for coming.)
- "Estos son los medicamentos que tomé." (These are the medicines I took.)
- "Soy alérgico / alérgica a..." (I am allergic to...)
- "¿Habla inglés?" (Do you speak English?)
- "Más despacio, por favor." (Slower, please.)
- "¿Puede escribirlo?" (Can you write it down?)
- "Me duele aquí." (It hurts here.)
- "Desde anoche." / "Desde hace dos días." (Since last night. / For two days.)
- "Tengo fiebre." (I have a fever.)
- "No puedo retener líquidos." (I cannot keep fluids down.)
- "Estoy embarazada." (I am pregnant.)

## At the pharmacy (droguería)
- "Tengo una fórmula médica." (I have a prescription.)
- "¿Tienen servicio a domicilio?" (Do you deliver?)
- "¿Cuánto cuesta?" (How much is it?)
- Pharmacists in Colombia can be asked about medicines, but follow the doctor's instructions on what to take and how much.

## Useful words
- Urgencias: emergency department.
- Droguería: pharmacy.
- Fórmula médica: prescription.
- Suero oral: oral rehydration solution.
- Póliza: insurance policy.
- Cita: appointment.
- Fiebre: fever. Vómito: vomiting. Diarrea: diarrhea. Mareo: dizziness. Dolor: pain.
